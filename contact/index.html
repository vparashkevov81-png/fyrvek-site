Questions

Frequently asked

What exactly is the Journey Audit?
A structured review of your store, ads, pages, and email flows, mapped against the path your customer takes. You receive a findings document with the five improvements most likely to lift conversion first, delivered within five working days. If it surfaces nothing you consider worth fixing, you keep it and pay nothing.
Who will actually do the work?
The person you are writing to. FYRVEK is a founder led practice: strategy, copy, and delivery come from one specialist, not a rotating team.
Do you run Meta ads or manage media buying?
No. FYRVEK builds the creative and the journey around the ads: concepts, scripts, pages, and email. Media buying stays with your buyer or agency, and the work is built to make their job easier.
How fast do projects move?
The audit takes five working days. Sprints run one to three weeks depending on scope. Timelines are agreed in writing before work starts and include your review rounds.
How do we communicate?
In writing, async by default: documents, Loom walkthroughs, and email or Slack. You get answers you can reread and forward, and timezone differences stop mattering. Calls are scheduled when a decision genuinely needs one.
Do you use AI tools?
Yes, the way a good studio uses any modern tool: to move faster on research and production. Strategy, judgement, and final quality are human, and you are buying the outcome, not the output volume.
What does it cost?
The Journey Audit is a small fixed fee, stated on its page, and it is credited toward any sprint booked within 30 days. Sprint and partnership pricing is quoted individually once the audit shows the real scope.
What if we are not on Shopify?
The system thinking transfers to any DTC stack, but Shopify and Klaviyo are where the practice is sharpest. If your setup is far from that, the honest answer may be a referral instead of a proposal.
