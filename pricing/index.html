Questions

About pricing

Why are the launch prices lower?
FYRVEK is new, and the first projects are where I earn reviews. The work, the scope and the standard are the same as at the regular price. After the first 5 projects, the regular prices apply.
Is the Klaviyo subscription included?
No. Klaviyo bills your brand directly, and its free plan covers up to 250 active profiles. My price covers the strategy, copy, design and build.
Can I combine services?
Yes. Tell me what you need, and you get one written quote with a combined scope and one timeline.
What if my store needs something different?
Every quote is made for your store. If what you need is smaller or larger than a package, the quote says so before anything starts.
How do we work together?
Everything happens in writing, by email or Slack. No calls needed: every decision stays documented, and I reply within 24 hours on working days.
