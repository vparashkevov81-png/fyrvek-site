Questions before you start

What exactly do I get?
A sign-up pop-up and a 4-email welcome flow, written, designed and built in your Klaviyo account, tested and live within 14 days. The full list is in What you get and on the starter offer page.
Who will actually do the work?
I will. I write, design and build everything myself, and you talk to me directly.
How much does it cost?
The starter offer is $390 at launch price for my first 5 projects (regular $490). See all prices on the pricing page. Every store is different, so every project is quoted individually: you tell me what you need, and within 24 hours you get a written offer with a fixed price, scope and dates. No hourly billing and no surprises.
What do I need to have before we start?
A Shopify store with Klaviyo connected (any Klaviyo plan, including the free one), your logo and brand colours, a few product photos, and the welcome offer you want to give, for example 10% off the first order.
How do we work together?
By email or Slack, in writing. You get each draft as a document or a Klaviyo preview link, leave your comments, and I make the changes. No calls needed: every decision stays in writing, so nothing gets lost. I reply within 24 hours on working days.
How do I pay?
50% when we start and 50% when the flow is ready to go live, by card through a secure payment link or by bank transfer. Before we start, you get a short written offer that lists the scope, dates and price.
Who owns the work?
You do. Once the final payment is made, the copy, designs and everything built in your Klaviyo account belong to your brand.
