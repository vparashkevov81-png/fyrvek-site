SUNFOLD: abandoned cart, browse abandonment, launch and segmentation

Abandoned cart flow · Browse abandonment flow · Launch campaign · Segments · A/B tests · 7 emails

A full Klaviyo build for a skincare brand. The abandoned cart flow splits by cart value, so shoppers under $45 get a free-shipping code and shoppers over $45 are reminded that shipping is already free. HALDEN Sport: welcome, replenishment and Black Friday emails

Welcome flow · Replenishment flow · BFCM campaign · 8 emails

Eight emails for a sports supplement brand, across three moments of the customer journey: a welcome flow that turns new subscribers into a first order, a replenishment flow timed to when the tub runs out, and a Black Friday campaign with early access for subscribers. Every email follows supplement compliance rules, and each one comes with a note on why it works.

Built in Klaviyo

All eight emails are set up in Klaviyo, not just written. A sign-up pop-up feeds the welcome flow, which stops as soon as someone buys. The replenishment flow starts on each Whey Isolate order and sends at 10 AM in the customer's time zone. Black Friday early access goes only to engaged subscribers, so the main sale lands in more inboxes.
